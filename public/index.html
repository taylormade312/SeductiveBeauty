TRUST & SAFETY

Platform policies

Platform safety and policy summary. Formal legal review is still required before enabling paid transactions.

Terms of Service

Users must be 18+, provide accurate account information, respect creator rights, and comply with applicable law. Monetized creator accounts require identity and age verification. This draft must be reviewed by qualified counsel before launch.

Privacy

Seductive Beauty should minimize data collection, disclose processors, secure account and billing data, provide deletion/access workflows, and publish retention practices. Payment data should be handled by an approved processor rather than stored directly.

Community Guidelines

No minors, non-consensual content, trafficking, exploitation, impersonation, doxxing, threats, illegal services, or prohibited material. Reporting, blocking, rapid review, evidence preservation, and law-enforcement escalation procedures are required.

Consent & Creator Safety

Creators must confirm ownership, participant consent, age eligibility, and recordkeeping for every upload. A production launch needs robust KYC, age verification, moderation, hash-matching/detection systems, and an abuse-response team.
