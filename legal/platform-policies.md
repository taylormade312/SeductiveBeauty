Seductive Beauty is an adult creator platform, but “adult” does not mean “anything goes.” The following rules apply to public posts, paid content, messages, profiles, usernames, comments, links, and other Platform activity.

## 1. Zero-Tolerance Safety Prohibitions

The following are prohibited:

- child sexual abuse material, sexual content involving minors, sexualization of minors, or content portraying a person as a minor in a sexual context;

- trafficking, sexual exploitation, slavery, coercion, extortion, or content produced through force, fraud, threat, or incapacity;
- non-consensual sexual activity or non-consensual intimate imagery;
- hidden-camera sexual content or content captured where a depicted person reasonably expected privacy and did not consent to distribution;
- credible threats, stalking, doxxing, swatting, blackmail, or encouragement of real-world violence;
- bestiality or sexual content involving animals;
- sexual content involving actual incest between close family members;
- content depicting real severe injury, torture, or sexual violence where consent is absent or cannot reasonably be established; and
- attempts to evade safety review by mislabeling, obscuring, encrypting, or moving prohibited transactions off-platform.

## 5. Fraud and Manipulation

No phishing, malware, account theft, fabricated identity documents, fake verification records, manipulated evidence, payment fraud, chargeback fraud, deceptive fundraising, or schemes designed to mislead users about what they are purchasing.

Possible actions include warning, visibility limits, content removal, feature restrictions, demonetization, account suspension, termination, evidence preservation, and referral to relevant authorities or service providers where legally appropriate.

# Part IV — Content, Consent & Recordkeeping Policy

## 1. Creator Responsibility

Creators are responsible for proving that every person appearing in regulated content is legally eligible to participate and gave informed, voluntary consent to creation and distribution.

## 2. Releases

Creators must maintain a release or equivalent legally sufficient authorization for every additional performer. The Platform may require a release record to be uploaded to a private compliance-records area. Release records are not public content.

## 3. Federal Recordkeeping

Where 18 U.S.C. §§ 2257 or 2257A and implementing regulations apply, persons treated as producers must satisfy applicable identification, recordkeeping, labeling, and inspection requirements. A checkbox, typed name, creator profile, or Platform self-attestation does not substitute for legally required examination of identification documents or maintenance of required records.

The Platform’s backend therefore treats age/identity status and federal recordkeeping review as separate compliance fields. Explicit content cannot be published through the production compliance workflow until the required statuses are cleared.

## 4. Content Ratings

Content may be categorized as non-explicit, suggestive, or explicit for moderation and access-control purposes. A rating does not determine whether a legal recordkeeping obligation applies; creators remain responsible for applicable law.

## 5. Consent Withdrawal and Safety Complaints

A performer or rights holder may report content alleged to be non-consensual or unauthorized. The Platform may immediately restrict access while investigating. Evidence such as release records, communications, source files, and account logs may be preserved for review.

## 6. Falsification

Submitting false consent, release, performer, age, identity, or recordkeeping information is a serious violation and may result in termination, preservation of evidence, withholding of affected content from publication, and referral where legally required.

# Part VI — Complaints, Takedowns & Appeals Policy

## 1. Reporting

Users may report accounts, posts, or messages for safety, consent, impersonation, harassment, copyright, or other policy concerns. Reports should be made in good faith and include enough information for review.

## 2. Emergency and Non-Consensual Content

Reports involving minors, trafficking, credible threats, or non-consensual sexual content receive heightened priority. The Platform may restrict content immediately while preserving relevant evidence.

## 3. Moderation Decisions

Where feasible, an affected user will receive notice of a moderation action and the general policy basis. Notice may be limited where disclosure would create a safety risk, compromise an investigation, violate law, expose another person’s private information, or undermine fraud/security controls.

## 4. Appeals

Eligible moderation actions may be appealed through the Platform. Appeals should identify the action and explain why it was incorrect or disproportionate. A reviewer may uphold, modify, or reverse the action. Emergency restrictions may remain during review.

## 5. Privacy Appeals

Where applicable privacy law provides an appeal right, a denied privacy request may be appealed. If a Texas privacy appeal is denied, the response should explain how the requester may contact the Texas Attorney General as required by applicable law.

## 6. Support

General support tickets are tracked separately from legal notices, privacy requests, and safety reports so that each request can be routed and retained appropriately.
